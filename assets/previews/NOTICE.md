# Preview images

icon are generated by [`scripts/build-assets.py`](../../scripts/build-assets.py)
from the committed clips in [`audio/`](../../audio) and the
[SCV atlas](../../pets/scv/spritesheet.webp). Images derived from the pet
artwork carry its CC BY 4.0 license ([license](../../pets/scv/LICENSE.txt));
credit "SCV pet artwork by Michael J. Mitchell / mitchtech". The waveforms
depict *StarCraft* audio, whose rights remain with Blizzard Entertainment; see
the [audio rights notice](../../audio/NOTICE.md). The mineral crystal and
faction emblems are original drawings, not Blizzard artwork.
